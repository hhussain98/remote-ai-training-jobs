## What is AI training work?

AI companies pay people to check and correct what their models write. The work draws on your own job: a nurse
checks medical answers, a lawyer checks legal ones, a developer checks code. Typical tasks:

- **Rating answers.** Compare two model responses, pick the better one and say why.
- **Writing the ideal answer.** The response the model should have given, used as training data.
- **Writing rubrics.** The checklist a good answer in your field has to meet.
- **Red-teaming.** Try to make a model give a wrong or unsafe answer, and record how.
- **Paid interviews and studies.** A single session about how you do your job.

Most of it is remote contract work. Today's hourly roles pay $2–450 an hour. Another 54 pay a fixed fee per session or task.

## How this list works

- **Every figure comes from the posting.** If a posting states no pay, we say so. We never estimate.
- **Links go to our pages.** Each one shows the full posting, who can apply, and how.
- **We are independent.** We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any platform listed. If you apply
  through our site and are hired, the platform may pay us a referral fee. Your pay is unaffected.
- **Rebuilt every 3 days** from the same data as [redpenjobs.com](https://redpenjobs.com/). To report a mistake, open an issue.
