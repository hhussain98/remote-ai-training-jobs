## What is AI training work?

AI companies pay people to check, correct and improve what their models produce. You are not doing your job for a
client: you are showing a model how someone in your job would do it. Typical tasks:

- **Rating and ranking answers** — compare two model responses and say which is better, and why.
- **Writing the ideal answer** — the response the model should have given, used as training data.
- **Rubric writing** — the checklist a good answer in your field must meet.
- **Red-teaming** — trying to make a model give an unsafe or wrong answer, and recording how.
- **Paid interviews and studies** — a one-off session about how you work.

Most work is remote, contract and paid per hour, session or task. Hourly roles listed today pay $5–450 an hour, as the postings state it; 60 more pay a fixed fee per session or task.

## How this list works

- **Every figure comes from the posting.** If a posting states no pay, we say so. We never estimate.
- **Links go to our pages,** which show the full posting, who it is open to, and how to apply.
- **We are independent.** We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any platform listed. If you apply
  through our site and are hired, the platform may pay us a referral fee. Your pay is unaffected.
- **Regenerated every week** from the same data as [redpenjobs.com](https://redpenjobs.com/). Spotted something wrong? Open an issue.
